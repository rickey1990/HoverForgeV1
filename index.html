Engineering rules
Engineering rules: banking follows signed curvature, with a strict 30° cap. The road profile never changes size; both glass shoulders always curve upward. A geometrically straight road has zero bank and gradient. Ramp rise/drop is limited to 3° (design target 2.25°), with long curved approaches and flat plateaus. Track scale is increased if radius or ramp space requires it; impossible crossings are rejected rather than erased. Orb supports are screened against other roadway and supports.
03Optional race pieces
Optional: you can race without these pieces. Build the track first, select a piece below, then tap the route in the drawing above. Place one START + PIT module on a straight or very gentle curve — it adds the grid, three start lights, launch spot and a pit lane. Boost tunnels are optional.

Module details
Fixed module: 700 m main straight or very gentle bend, 26 m pit centre offset, 17 m driving width plus shoulders. The pit has full-width barriers and a long shield-charging section. You only enter the pit by deliberately steering into it. Pick the side before placing; Flip 180° sends the field the opposite way round.

Choose 1–10 craft in total (including you). Nine rivals use pilot-matched bright paint, smooth passing lanes, individual shields/hulls, pit repairs and eliminations. Choose 1 car for solo.

Both held = straight throttle · one held = throttle + turn · neither = coast · DRIFT + direction = sharp turn. Keyboard: A/D or ←/→, Space for drift. Controller: L1/R1 (or L2/R2, D-pad) drive, Square for drift, Cross for boost — all rebindable in the controller box. Press B or tap BOOST while inside a boost tunnel.

Lower = smoother turns · 100% = original V30 steering. Drift still allows sharp corners.
