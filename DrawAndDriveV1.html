Draw a circuit. Generate a landscape. Drive it.

Draw a loop → Build & check → Drive. Daylight scenery, safe earth-cut ramps and concrete overpasses are built from your drawn road. Gentle banking, continuous barriers and a 200 mph top speed. The pit is now a real refuelling stop; boost tunnels and sci-fi supports are gone.

Safe driving: The asphalt is wider, with red-and-white kerbs, grass run-off that slows you down, and outer crash barriers that damage the car. Pit forks have separate lane boundaries and a roadside fuel service bay.

Save your work: use Save project to download an editable JSON file. Undo reverses your last edit.

Engineering rules
Engineering rules: banking follows signed curvature, with a gentle 8° cap and gradual transitions. Top speed is 200 mph. The asphalt is 19.5m wide; each side has a raised kerb, driveable grass run-off and an outer crash barrier. A geometrically straight road has zero bank and gradient. Ramp rise/drop is limited to 3° (design target 2.25°), with long curved approaches and flat plateaus. Track scale is increased if radius or ramp space requires it; impossible crossings are rejected rather than erased. Concrete bridge piers are placed in pairs outside the road edges, avoiding lower roads.
03Optional race pieces
Optional: you can race without these pieces. Build the track first, select a piece below, then tap the route in the drawing above. Place one START + PIT module on a straight or very gentle curve — it adds the grid, three start lights, launch spot and a pit lane. The service lane refuels the car at a roadside pump bay.

Module details
Fixed module: 700 m main straight or very gentle bend, 26 m pit centre offset, 19.5 m asphalt, red-and-white kerbs, grass run-off and outer barriers. The pit has kerbs, grass run-off, outer barriers and a marked refuelling service bay. You only enter the pit by deliberately steering into it. Pick the side before placing; Flip 180° sends the field the opposite way round.

Tap a slot, then press the pad button you want. START / OPTIONS starts a run. Left stick also steers.

Solo car by default. Other racing cars are not needed for the road and landscape prototype.

Both held = straight throttle · one held = throttle + turn · neither = coast · DRIFT + direction = sharp turn. Keyboard: A/D or ←/→, Space for drift. Controller: L1/R1 (or L2/R2, D-pad) to drive, Square for drift. Fuel is replenished in the pit lane, not by boost tunnels.

28% default: short taps make small corrections, not big sweeps. Hold to turn gradually; DRIFT + direction makes a tighter corner. Increase this slider for faster steering.
